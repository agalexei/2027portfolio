About me

I like getting close enough to a problem to see what’s making the next step hard.

I’m Ara Giulambarian, a Senior Experience Designer based in Sydney with 5+ years of experience across banking, insurance and e-commerce. Before moving into design, I worked in insurance broking at Aon. My Bachelor of Global Business at Macquarie University covered global business strategy, international operations and how businesses develop new ventures across markets. That background still shapes the questions I ask about how a service works for both the people who use it and the business that delivers it.

What I’m working on now

At Westpac, I own design for the credit memo and an integration on BizEdge, our business lending platform. I work with bankers, credit managers, product, engineering and risk to find where the process slows down and agree on what to build and test. I also build reusable AI workflows for design teams. Colleagues now use one of them to structure presentations and draft slides. I’m interested in where those tools can help us make sense of research or explain a decision more clearly.

What that looks like in practice

At IAG, I led the redesign of NRMA’s Home Insurance Quote & Buy journey after research showed that customers struggled to work out how much cover they needed. We simplified those decisions and made cover options easier to compare. Time to quote halved in the Building Only flow, and selection of higher-value cover increased by 75% compared with the previous experience.

Outside work, I’m usually at the gym, with family and friends, or planning a trip somewhere I can ski or snorkel.
