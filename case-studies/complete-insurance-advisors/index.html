The original CIANSW website build

I led the design and build of the original Complete Insurance Advisers NSW website in 2023. I used interviews and a review of competing sites to organise the experience around finding services, exploring cover and getting claims help.

The brief Research Design and build Delivery and measurement

Give an emerging brokerage a clear starting point online

Complete Insurance Advisers NSW needed a website that could explain what the brokerage did for individuals and businesses. I led the work from research and information architecture through interface design and front-end development. The main questions were what services were available, where to look for relevant cover and where to go for claims support.

Use interviews to put the market review in context

I reviewed competing brokerage sites and ran a SWOT analysis to understand where the experience could improve. User interviews pointed to three recurring difficulties: finding a way around brokerage sites, understanding service descriptions and locating claims support. Those findings gave me practical tasks to design around.

Translate those tasks into the site structure

Finding a service
I organised the content into clearer service categories so visitors had a starting point for exploring what the brokerage could help with.
Understanding the offer
I used a clearer visual hierarchy and more structured service descriptions to make the content easier to scan.
Getting claims help
I used more direct navigation to give claims support a clear place in the site structure.

I prototyped and built the responsive front end in HTML, CSS and Bootstrap, carrying the same content hierarchy across devices.

What shipped and what I planned to measure

The 2023 build established the brokerage’s online presence and brought services, cover information and claims support together. I identified engagement, navigation and conversion paths as measures to track after launch. I also planned follow-up user testing to see where visitors still needed help.

The later CIANSW redesign revisited that foundation with a more task-focused approach across six core routes.
